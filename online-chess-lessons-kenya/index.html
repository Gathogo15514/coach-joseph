Online lessons remove the commute without removing the coach. Each session is live, with a shared board, a clear plan and homework until the next one.

I teach students across Kenya online, as well as children abroad, including in Malaysia. Lessons run on Lichess or Chess.com, both free to join, with video so we can talk through every move together.

How online lessons work

  • Before the lesson you share recent games through your Lichess or Chess.com username, so the session starts from real mistakes, not generic theory.
  • During the lesson we work on a shared board: tactics, openings, endgames or a full game review, depending on the plan agreed for that month.
  • After the lesson you get puzzles and practice games, and the next session begins by checking how they went.

Who online coaching suits

Busy families who cannot fit travel into the week, students outside Nairobi, adult improvers who want to train in the evening, and players preparing for a tournament who need extra sessions at short notice.

Online lessons are KES 1,000 per session on every programme, and the first session is 25% off.

Common questions

What do I need for an online chess lesson?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a stable internet connection, and a free Lichess or Chess.com account.

Can you teach students outside Kenya?

Yes. I already teach students abroad. We agree a time that works across time zones.

How much are online chess lessons?

KES 1,000 per session on any programme, with 25% off the first session.

Are online lessons as effective as in person?

For most students, yes. Working on a shared board with your own games makes the lesson very focused. Many students combine both.
