"Coach" includes any assistant coach, arbiter or partner coach engaged by Joseph Gathogo. "Parent" is the signing parent or guardian, responsible for the Student and for all fees. "Written notice" is a WhatsApp message or email to the contacts above, counted from when it is received.

The agreement covers every Session and Event until ended under Section 10. Where a school or club has its own contract with the Coach, that contract governs fees and scheduling, and this policy governs conduct, safety, media and liability. The Coach may update this policy with 14 days' written notice; continuing after that date means the changes are accepted. If one clause is unenforceable, the rest still applies.

2. Services, fees and payment

Fees are charged per Session at the rates in the Coach's current written fee schedule. Coaching does not include tournament entry fees, federation or rating fees, transport, meals, accommodation or other Event costs; these are paid by the Parent. No results, ratings, titles or selection are guaranteed.

  • Payment is by M-Pesa or bank transfer to the details on the invoice; cash only against a written receipt.
  • After a Session or block is confirmed, the Parent pays in full or the agreed deposit; any balance is due within 7 days of the invoice date.
  • Packages are paid in advance and must be used within the period on the invoice. Unused Sessions expire without refund, except where the Coach cancels.
  • Invoices unpaid 14 days after the due date may lead to Sessions and Event support being suspended. Reasonable recovery costs may be added.
  • Fee changes are notified in writing at least 14 days in advance. Fees paid are non-refundable except as set out in Sections 3 and 10.

3. Cancellations, rescheduling and missed sessions

Any Session cancelled with less than 24 hours' written notice is invoiced and payable in full.

  • 24 hours' written notice or more: no charge; Session moved to an agreed time.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice, no-show or no notice: 100% of the Session fee invoiced.
  • Late arrival or late log-in: full fee; the Session ends at the scheduled time.
  • Student sent home for misconduct: full fee.
  • Coach cancels: no charge; Session rescheduled or credited.
  • Event entries cancelled after the organiser's deadline are not refunded by the Coach.

Notice must be written (WhatsApp 0746 451 222 or chess1elite@gmail.com); messages passed through the Student, a driver or school staff do not count. The 24 hours are counted back from the start time. Illness, travel, school activities, exams, family events, traffic and weather are not exceptions; the Coach may waive a charge in a genuine emergency at his sole discretion, and one waiver creates no right to another. Three or more late cancellations or no-shows in a month may lead to the regular slot being released. The Coach waits 15 minutes before treating a Session as a no-show. A Student not collected on time may be charged a supervision fee for each extra 30 minutes at half the hourly rate. The Coach may cancel for illness, emergencies, Event duties, unsafe conditions or power/internet failure, with the Session rescheduled or credited and no other liability.

4. Conduct, safeguarding and supervision

Sessions take place in open, visible settings. The Coach will not be alone with a Student behind a closed door, will not transport a Student alone except as agreed in writing for an Event, communicates through the Parent or groups that include parents, and keeps any contact appropriate. Safeguarding concerns are reported to the Parent and, where the law requires, to the school or authorities.

The Student agrees to respect everyone; follow the FIDE Laws of Chess and fair play; never use engines, phones or outside help where not allowed; look after equipment (deliberate or careless damage is charged at replacement cost); stay in agreed areas; and avoid bullying, fighting, abusive language, theft or dangerous behaviour. Breaches may lead to a warning, removal, collection by the Parent or ending coaching, with fees still payable.

The Parent drops off and collects on time (the Coach's duty of care runs from the start to the end of the Session), keeps contacts current and stays reachable, behaves respectfully, does not coach or signal a Student during play at Events, and tells the Coach about anything affecting safety or learning. Only the Parent or people authorised in this form may collect the Student; the Coach may refuse anyone else. Where the Parent allows the Student to leave alone, the Coach has no responsibility once the Student leaves the venue.

5. Health, medical care and emergencies

The Parent discloses any condition, allergy, medication or special need and updates the Coach in writing. The Coach is not a medical professional and does not give medication; a Student needing medication carries and takes it with written instructions. In an emergency the Coach first tries the Parent and emergency contacts; if no one can be reached quickly, the Parent authorises the Coach to arrange first aid, call an ambulance, and take or send the Student to the nearest suitable hospital, and authorises qualified medical staff to give necessary treatment. All medical and related costs are paid by the Parent or their insurer, and anything the Coach pays is repaid within 7 days. The Parent keeps suitable medical insurance. Medical information is shared only where needed for safety.

6. Tournaments, travel and trips

The Coach takes or accompanies Students to Events only with consent in this form and a separate event form for each Event outside Nairobi or involving an overnight stay. Parents are welcome to accompany their child.

  • The Coach shares Event details, travel plan and costs in writing. The Parent confirms in writing and pays all Event costs in advance, including the Coach's Event support fee where it applies.
  • The Parent gives correct registration details and any required documents. The Coach is not responsible for disqualification or rating errors caused by wrong or late information. Entry fees follow the organiser's refund rules.
  • Travel may be by public transport, hired licensed vehicle, school or club bus, organiser transport or a consenting parent's vehicle. The Coach is not a transport operator and is not responsible for road and travel risks or the acts of drivers, operators or other road users. Where the Parent arranges transport, the Coach's responsibility runs only from check-in at the venue to collection or the end of the Event.
  • The Coach provides reasonable group supervision but cannot watch each Student every minute; during play Students are under the arbiters and organisers. Organisers, arbiters and venues are responsible for their own premises, rules, food and security.
  • Overnight: Students share rooms only with Students of the same sex or their own Parent; the Coach never shares a room with a Student; where possible another adult travels; curfew applies; alcohol, tobacco, vaping, drugs and weapons are prohibited; the Parent provides spending money.
  • A Student who breaks the code of conduct, puts anyone at risk or becomes too unwell may be sent home; the Parent collects or authorises the Coach to arrange the safest return, at the Parent's cost, without refund of Event fees.
  • The Coach is not liable for costs caused by organisers changing or cancelling Events, but passes on any refund received, and may withdraw a Student or cancel a trip where conditions are unsafe.

7. Online coaching

The Parent provides a working device, internet, power and platform accounts. An adult should be within reach during online Sessions for Students under 13. Time lost to problems on the Student's side is not made up and is charged; time lost on the Coach's side is made up or credited. Sessions are not recorded without written agreement. The Coach is not responsible for contact with other users on public platforms outside Sessions, or for platform outages.

The Coach is not responsible for schools, clubs, venues, hotels, organisers, arbiters, transport operators, caterers, online platforms, other players or other parents. The Parent indemnifies the Coach against claims and costs arising from the Student's behaviour or deliberate damage, false or incomplete information given by the Parent, the Parent's breach of this agreement, or any released claim brought by or for the Student. Coaching advice is given in good faith with no guarantee of results.

9. Photos, media, privacy and materials

Photos and videos are used only as consented in this form, with first names only when published; home address, timetable and contact details are never published. Consent can be withdrawn in writing; published posts the Coach controls are taken down on request. At public Events, organisers, media and spectators may publish photos, broadcasts, games and results outside the Coach's control.

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, the Coach collects only what is needed to coach, register for Events, invoice and keep the Student safe; keeps it securely; shares it only with organisers, federations, schools, medical staff or payment providers where needed; never sells it; and deletes it up to 2 years after coaching ends unless the law requires longer. The Parent may ask to see, correct or delete it in writing. Progress records may be kept on the Coach's coaching tools, including ChessLead. All coaching materials remain the Coach's property, for the Student's personal use only.

10. Ending coaching, disputes and general terms

Either side may end coaching with 7 days' written notice; fees for Sessions held, late-cancelled or inside the notice period remain payable. Parent notice: unused prepaid Sessions may be used within the notice period but are not refunded. Coach notice: unused prepaid Sessions are refunded in proportion. The Coach may end coaching immediately for serious misconduct, abusive or threatening behaviour, non-payment beyond 30 days, or risk to others. Sections 2 (unpaid fees), 8, 9 and 10 survive.

Concerns are raised in writing first, with a response within 7 days; if not resolved within 14 days, both sides try mediation in Nairobi before court. Kenyan law governs, and the courts of Kenya (Nairobi) have jurisdiction. The Coach is not liable for failures caused by events beyond reasonable control. This policy, this form, the current fee schedule and any event form make up the whole agreement; verbal promises are not binding unless confirmed in writing.
