Terms for this event

These terms add to the Chess Coaching Policy signed in Form 1. If anything differs, the policy applies.

  1. Supervision. The coach supervises the group with reasonable care but cannot watch every child every minute. During games, children are under the control of the arbiters and organisers, who are responsible for the venue, its rules, food and security.
  2. Travel. Where travel is arranged by the coach, it may be by public transport, a hired vehicle with a licensed driver, a school or club bus, or organiser transport. The coach is not a transport operator. Road and travel risks, and the acts of drivers and other road users, are accepted by the parent.
  3. Medical. The coach will first try to reach you and your emergency contact. If no one can be reached quickly, you authorise the coach to arrange first aid, call an ambulance and take your child to the nearest suitable hospital or clinic. All medical costs are paid by the parent or the parent's insurer.
  4. Conduct. Your child must follow the instructions of the coach and arbiters, play fairly, never use phones or engines during games, and never leave the venue or group without permission. A child who breaks the rules or puts anyone at risk may be sent home. You will collect them or pay for their safe return.
  5. Collection. Only the person named in this form may collect your child. The coach's responsibility ends when your child is handed over or the event ends, whichever comes first. Please be on time.
  6. Belongings. Phones, money, chess sets and other belongings are brought at your child's own risk.
  7. Results and photos. Organisers, federations and media may publish results, pairings, games, photos and live broadcasts. This is outside the coach's control.
  8. Overnight stay. Children share rooms only with children of the same sex or their own parent. The coach never shares a room with a child. A curfew applies. Alcohol, tobacco, vaping, drugs and weapons are forbidden.
  9. Changes and safety. Organisers may change or cancel the event. The coach may withdraw a child, or cancel travel, if conditions are unsafe, and is not liable for costs this causes.
  10. Liability. To the fullest extent allowed by Kenyan law, you accept the ordinary risks of this event and its travel, and release the coach and his assistants from claims connected with it, except where caused by the coach's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.
