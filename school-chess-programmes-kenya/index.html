A good school chess programme gives every child a reason to think carefully, and gives the school a team that can compete at Chess Kenya championships.

I currently run chess for German School Nairobi, Strathmore School and Light International School in Lavington, and coach competitive juniors at PMadol Chess Club and Totus Elite Chess Club. I also organise and direct tournaments with Chess Kenya, so school teams arrive at events prepared for how they are run.

What the programme includes

  • Weekly lessons planned as a term-long syllabus, from first moves to tournament play.
  • Separate groups for beginners and the school team.
  • In-school tournaments and ladders so every student plays rated-style games.
  • Termly progress reports for parents and the school.
  • Preparation and entry support for Chess Kenya school and junior championships.

Common questions

Does our school need its own chess sets?

For schools outside Nairobi the school provides the boards and I bring all books and training materials. For Nairobi schools we agree what is needed when planning the programme.

Which schools do you coach at now?

German School Nairobi, Strathmore School and Light International School, plus PMadol Chess Club and Totus Elite Chess Club.

Can you prepare our team for Chess Kenya tournaments?

Yes. Tournament preparation and entry support for Chess Kenya school and junior championships are part of the programme.
